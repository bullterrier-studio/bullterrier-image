# Effects

Every effect, one page per category, each with a picture of it, its parameters and the example recipe
that made the picture. `uv run photo-fx effects` prints the same documentation.

Generated by `poe regenerate` from the effects themselves: do not edit by hand.

## Categories

| Category | Effects | What it holds |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [Colour](colour.md) | 20 | Grades, gradient maps, tints and hue changes: they recolour the picture and leave its shapes alone. |
| [Light](light.md) | 22 | Glow, bloom and local contrast: they add or shape the light in the picture. |
| [Texture](texture.md) | 10 | Grain and other surface texture laid over the picture. |
| [Stylised](stylised.md) | 4 | Looks that redraw the picture in a printing or drawing style, such as halftone. |
| [Text](text.md) | 1 | Pictures redrawn as characters, such as ASCII art. |
| [Distortion](distortion.md) | 15 | Warps that bend, ripple or displace the picture's geometry. |
| [Glitch](glitch.md) | 4 | Digital-error looks: displaced slices, colour splits and corrupted blocks. |
| [Atmosphere](atmosphere.md) | 8 | Weather and air: fog, haze, rain and the like. |
| [Subject](subject.md) | 8 | Effects that work on the cut-out subject, apart from its background. |
| [Backgrounds](backgrounds.md) | 9 | Generated or replaced backgrounds behind the subject. |
| [Animation](animation.md) | 2 | Effects that change over time, such as particles dispersing or evaporating. |

## All effects

- [`anamorphic_streaks`](light.md#anamorphic_streaks): Horizontal streaks of light: what is brighter than `threshold` is smeared along its row, tinted `color` and screened over the picture by `amount`.
- [`ascii`](text.md#ascii): Redraw the picture as text: a grid of characters `cell` px wide, each chosen by the brightness under it.
- [`aurora`](backgrounds.md#aurora): Replace the picture with an aurora: a curtain of light with a wavy base and vertical rays over a dark sky.
- [`auto_levels`](colour.md#auto_levels): Stretch the tones so the darkest values become black and the brightest white, ignoring `clip` at each end.
- [`black_and_white`](colour.md#black_and_white): Convert to black and white with `red`, `green` and `blue` filter weights, like a colour filter on B&W film.
- [`bloom`](light.md#bloom): Light bleeding from what is brighter than `threshold`, blurred at `octaves` scales and screened over the picture.
- [`bump_light`](texture.md#bump_light): Light the picture's brightness, read as a relief `height` px tall, from `angle` at `elevation`.
- [`channel_mixer`](colour.md#channel_mixer): Build each output channel as a weighted sum of the input red, green and blue, set by a 3x3 matrix of weights.
- [`chromatic_aberration`](light.md#chromatic_aberration): Shift red outward and blue inward from the centre `x`, `y` by up to `amount` px, leaving green alone.
- [`clarity`](light.md#clarity): Local contrast: exaggerate how each area differs from its surroundings, so features stand out even when the whole face sits in a narrow band of brightness.
- [`color_balance`](colour.md#color_balance): Shift red, green and blue separately in the shadows, the midtones and the highlights.
- [`curves`](colour.md#curves): Bend tones along smooth curves through control points: a master curve, then one per colour channel.
- [`dehaze`](light.md#dehaze): Remove haze and fog by inverting the atmospheric scattering model I = J t + A (1 - t) (He, Sun, Tang 2009).
- [`disperse`](animation.md#disperse): The picture at one moment of its dispersal: `progress` 0 = untouched, 1 = finished.
- [`displace`](distortion.md#displace): Move every pixel by two independent noise fields (down and right), the longest move being `amount` px.
- [`drop_shadow`](subject.md#drop_shadow): A soft shadow of the subject cast on the picture behind it, moved by `dx`, `dy` and softened by `blur`.
- [`dust_scratches`](texture.md#dust_scratches): Draw light and dark specks, hairs and scratches at random places, over the picture at `opacity`.
- [`echo`](glitch.md#echo): Lay `copies` translucent copies over the picture, each (`dx`, `dy`) px further from it and weaker by `fade`.
- [`embers`](atmosphere.md#embers): Screen `count` sparks of `color` at random places over the picture, each rising with a streak behind it.
- [`emboss`](texture.md#emboss): Read the brightness as a relief `depth` px tall and light it from `angle`.
- [`equalize`](colour.md#equalize): Equalise the brightness histogram tile by tile (CLAHE, Zuiderveld 1994), keeping each pixel's colour.
- [`evaporate`](animation.md#evaporate): Dissolve the picture into pieces that float away, thin out and fade.
- [`fade`](colour.md#fade): Lift the blacks by `amount` for a matte, faded-film look; whites stay where they are.
- [`fill`](subject.md#fill): Replace the whole picture with one colour.
- [`film_grain`](texture.md#film_grain): Film grain: noise per channel whose deviation runs from `shadows` at black to `highlights` at white.
- [`fisheye`](distortion.md#fisheye): Bend the picture as a fisheye lens with its axis at `x`, `y` would, magnifying the middle.
- [`floating_dust`](atmosphere.md#floating_dust): Screen `count` soft round specks of `color` at random places over the picture, as out-of-focus dust.
- [`flow_lines`](backgrounds.md#flow_lines): Replace the picture with streamlines of a noise flow: lines that start at random points and follow the flow.
- [`flow_warp`](distortion.md#flow_warp): Carry the picture `amount` px along a flow whose direction is `angle` plus `variation` degrees of fractal noise.
- [`fog`](atmosphere.md#fog): Mix the picture toward `color` by a fog opacity that is cloudy and heavier toward the bottom.
- [`fractal`](backgrounds.md#fractal): Replace the picture with the Mandelbrot set or a Julia set, coloured by how fast each point escapes.
- [`gaussian_blur`](light.md#gaussian_blur): Soften the picture with a Gaussian blur of standard deviation `radius` px.
- [`glitch`](glitch.md#glitch): `bands` random horizontal strips slide sideways by up to `shift` of the width, then red and blue are pushed `split` px apart.
- [`glow`](light.md#glow): Soft bloom around the bright parts: what is brighter than `threshold` is blurred by `radius` px and screened over the picture by `amount`.
- [`grade`](colour.md#grade): Basic tone controls: `exposure` in stops, then `contrast` around `pivot`, then `saturation`.
- [`gradient`](backgrounds.md#gradient): Replace the picture with a gradient through `colors`: a straight band, rings round a centre or a sweep.
- [`gradient_map`](colour.md#gradient_map): Recolour by brightness: darkest pixels get the first stop, brightest the last, evenly spaced between.
- [`grain`](texture.md#grain): Film grain: brightness noise of standard deviation `amount` (0.08 visible, 0.2 heavy), clumped to `size` px.
- [`halation`](light.md#halation): A coloured glow around the highlights: where the brightest channel is above `threshold` it is blurred by `radius` px, tinted `color` and screened over the picture by `amount`.
- [`halftone`](stylised.md#halftone): Print-style dot screen: tone becomes dot size.
- [`highpass`](light.md#highpass): Keep only the detail finer than `radius` px: the picture minus its Gaussian blur, centred on mid-grey.
- [`hue_shift`](colour.md#hue_shift): Rotate every hue around the colour wheel by `hue` degrees: the quickest way to try another colour scheme.
- [`invert`](colour.md#invert): Turn the picture into its negative: every red, green and blue value v becomes 1 - v.
- [`kaleidoscope`](distortion.md#kaleidoscope): Fold the picture about the centre `x`, `y` into `segments` sectors, each a wedge of the picture and its mirror.
- [`layered_cutouts`](subject.md#layered_cutouts): Copies of the subject's silhouette stacked behind it, each `offset` px farther along `angle`, like cut paper.
- [`lens_blur`](light.md#lens_blur): Blur the picture over a disc of `radius` px, as an out-of-focus lens does, favouring bright pixels.
- [`lens_distortion`](distortion.md#lens_distortion): Bend the picture as a lens with radial distortion `distortion` about the axis `x`, `y` does.
- [`lens_drops`](atmosphere.md#lens_drops): Put `count` water drops on the picture, each showing the blurred scene turned upside down by `refraction`.
- [`lens_flare`](light.md#lens_flare): A lens flare: a halo and a ring round the light at (`x`, `y`) and `ghosts` discs along the line through the picture's centre, tinted `color` and screened over the picture by `amount`.
- [`levels`](colour.md#levels): Remap tones with an input black point, white point and midtone `gamma`, then an output range.
- [`light_leak`](light.md#light_leak): Warm washes of light coming in from the side `angle` points to: `blobs` seeded patches, tinted `color` and screened over the picture by `amount`.
- [`light_rays`](light.md#light_rays): God rays: what is brighter than `threshold` is streaked away from (`x`, `y`) and screened over the picture by `amount`.
- [`liquid_metal`](stylised.md#liquid_metal): Chrome that follows the picture's brightness: its relief, `relief` px tall, mirrors a sky above a ground.
- [`long_shadow`](subject.md#long_shadow): A flat-design long shadow: the subject's silhouette repeated along `angle` for `length` px, behind it.
- [`melt`](distortion.md#melt): Pull the bright parts of each column downward, up to `amount` px, by a drip length that differs per column.
- [`mirror`](distortion.md#mirror): Make the picture symmetric: keep one half (or quarter) and replace the rest by its reflection.
- [`motion_blur`](light.md#motion_blur): Smear the picture along a straight line of `length` px at `angle` degrees, as a moving camera would.
- [`noise`](texture.md#noise): Add noise of standard deviation `amount` (0.06 visible, 0.2 heavy) to every pixel, grey or per channel.
- [`noise_field`](backgrounds.md#noise_field): Replace the picture with a noise field of `kind` (value, perlin or worley), coloured through `colors`.
- [`outline`](subject.md#outline): A coloured stroke of `width` px around the subject's silhouette, with an optional soft halo of `glow` px.
- [`palette_quantize`](colour.md#palette_quantize): Reduce the picture to a few colours: `colors` found in it by k-means, or a named `palette`.
- [`paper_texture`](texture.md#paper_texture): Lay a paper, canvas or linen texture over the picture: each pixel is multiplied by 1 + `amount` x texture.
- [`pattern`](backgrounds.md#pattern): Replace the picture with a repeating pattern of stripes, checks, dots or a grid in two colours.
- [`perspective`](distortion.md#perspective): Tilt the picture as a camera would see it: keystone it by `vertical` then `horizontal`, turn it, zoom it.
- [`pinch_bulge`](distortion.md#pinch_bulge): Push the picture out (`amount` above 0, a bulge) or in (below 0, a pinch) about the centre `x`, `y`.
- [`pixel_grid`](texture.md#pixel_grid): The picture on an LCD: each `cell` px cell split into red, green and blue stripes with a dark `gap` between.
- [`plasma`](backgrounds.md#plasma): Replace the picture with a plasma: warped, crossed sine waves coloured through `colors`.
- [`polar`](distortion.md#polar): Wrap the picture round the centre `x`, `y` into a planet (`mode` 'to'), or unwrap such a planet ('from').
- [`posterize`](colour.md#posterize): Reduce every colour channel to `levels` evenly spaced tones, each value snapping to the nearest one.
- [`radial_blur`](light.md#radial_blur): Smear the picture around a centre (`mode` 'spin') or away from it ('zoom'), by `amount`.
- [`rain`](atmosphere.md#rain): Screen falling streaks of `color` over the picture, `density` of them per 1000 px², leaning by `angle`.
- [`reaction_diffusion`](stylised.md#reaction_diffusion): Grow a Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion pattern (spots, worms, mazes) from the picture's bright parts.
- [`reflection`](subject.md#reflection): A floor mirror: the picture above the subject's lowest row is mirrored about it, softened and fading with depth.
- [`rgb_split`](glitch.md#rgb_split): Push the red and blue channels `amount` px apart along `angle`, leaving green where it is.
- [`rim_light`](subject.md#rim_light): Light along the subject's edge from one side: a band of `width` px inside the edge, tinted `color`.
- [`ripple`](distortion.md#ripple): Move every pixel by a sine wave of `wavelength` and `amplitude` that travels in the direction `angle`.
- [`scan_roll`](glitch.md#scan_roll): Roll the picture by `offset` of its height (`axis` 'vertical') or width ('horizontal'), as a slipped sync does.
- [`scanlines`](texture.md#scanlines): Dark horizontal lines every `spacing` px, each `thickness` x `spacing` thick, darkening by `darkness`.
- [`selective_color`](colour.md#selective_color): Shift the hue, saturation and lightness of the colours in one hue range, leaving every other pixel untouched.
- [`shadows_highlights`](light.md#shadows_highlights): Lighten the dark areas and darken the bright ones of the picture, as judged by an edge-aware local brightness.
- [`sharpen`](light.md#sharpen): Sharpen with an unsharp mask: add `amount` times the detail that a `radius` px Gaussian blur removes.
- [`shear`](distortion.md#shear): Skew the picture about its centre: slide rows sideways by `x` px per row, then columns down by `y` px per column.
- [`slice_shift`](distortion.md#slice_shift): Cut the picture into horizontal strips at random rows and slide each sideways by a random distance.
- [`smoke`](atmosphere.md#smoke): Mix soft-edged plumes of `color` into the picture, up to `amount` thick.
- [`snow`](atmosphere.md#snow): Screen white flakes over the picture, `density` of them per 1000 px², in `layers` depth layers.
- [`soft_focus`](light.md#soft_focus): A dreamy glow over the whole picture: a copy blurred by `radius` px is screened over the sharp one.
- [`solarize`](colour.md#solarize): Invert every red, green and blue value that is above `threshold` and leave the others as they are.
- [`sparkle`](atmosphere.md#sparkle): Screen sparkles over `count` random pixels brighter than `threshold`: a soft dot with four rays each.
- [`split_tone`](colour.md#split_tone): Tint the dark parts with `shadow` and the bright parts with `highlight`, without changing brightness.
- [`spotlight`](subject.md#spotlight): Light the subject and darken the rest: the further from the silhouette, the more the picture goes to `color`.
- [`starburst`](light.md#starburst): Diffraction spikes: what is brighter than `threshold` gets `rays` thin lines of light, tinted `color` and screened over the picture by `amount`.
- [`starfield`](backgrounds.md#starfield): A night sky of `count` stars of `color` over `sky`: a generated picture that replaces the input.
- [`swirl`](distortion.md#swirl): Twist the picture about the centre `x`, `y` by `angle` degrees there, falling to nothing at `radius`.
- [`threshold`](colour.md#threshold): Turn the picture into a two-colour bitmap: `light` where its brightness reaches `level`, `dark` elsewhere.
- [`tile`](distortion.md#tile): Repeat the whole picture, scaled down, in a grid of `columns` by `rows`.
- [`tilt_shift`](light.md#tilt_shift): A miniature look: the band `width` wide at `focus` stays sharp, and away from it the picture is blurred up to `radius` and its colour raised by `saturation`.
- [`tone_map`](light.md#tone_map): Compress the broad brightness differences of the picture and keep (or boost) its fine detail.
- [`topographic`](stylised.md#topographic): Draw contour lines where the picture's smoothed brightness crosses `levels` evenly spaced heights.
- [`torn_edge`](texture.md#torn_edge): Tear the border away: each side's pixels closer than a wandering distance of about `depth` px become `fill`.
- [`vibrance`](colour.md#vibrance): Raise the saturation of muted colours more than that of vivid ones, never lowering anyone's.
- [`vignette`](light.md#vignette): Fade the picture toward `color` from `radius` out to its corners, the centre untouched.
- [`voronoi_cells`](backgrounds.md#voronoi_cells): Replace the picture with a Voronoi diagram: random cells filled from a colour ramp, with thin borders.
- [`white_balance`](colour.md#white_balance): Shift the colour balance by `temperature` (warm-cool) and `tint` (green-magenta), in stops of linear light.
